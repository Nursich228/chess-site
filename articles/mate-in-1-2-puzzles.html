Mate in 1 and mate in 2 puzzles with answers

Mating puzzles are the best way to learn to see checks and a weak king. Try to solve each puzzle yourself, then open the answer: it explains why the move is best.

How to solve

In every puzzle it is White to move. Check all checks first, then all captures: almost every mate starts with a check.

Mate in 1

Puzzle 1

Show the answer

Solution: 1. ♖e8#

  1. ♖e8# — The rook goes from e1 to e8. Checkmate! The king can't escape, and the check can't be blocked or captured. It mates at once — no move can be stronger.

Result: checkmate.

Mate in 2

Puzzle 4

Show the answer

Solution: 1. ♖d8+ ♘e8 2. ♖xe8#

  1. ♖d8+ — The rook goes from d1 to d8. Check — the opponent has only one reply: ♘e8. It leads to a forced mate: the opponent has no defence. The solution is checked by an engine: other moves miss the win.
  2. ♘e8 — Gets out of check. It is the only move.
  3. ♖xe8# — The rook takes the knight on e8. Checkmate! The king can't escape, and the check can't be blocked or captured. It mates at once — no move can be stronger.

Result: checkmate.

Puzzle 5

Show the answer

Solution: 1. ♖f8+ ♖xf8 2. exf8=♕#

  1. ♖f8+ — The rook goes from f3 to f8. Check — the opponent has only one reply: ♖xf8. Fork: the rook attacks the king and the rook at the same time. It leads to a forced mate: the opponent has no defence. The solution is checked by an engine: other moves miss the win.
  2. ♖xf8 — Takes the rook on f8. It is the only move.
  3. exf8=♕# — The pawn takes the rook on f8. The pawn reaches the last rank and promotes to a queen. Checkmate! The king can't escape, and the check can't be blocked or captured. It mates at once — no move can be stronger.

Result: checkmate.
