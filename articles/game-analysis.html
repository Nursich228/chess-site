Chess game analysis online: how to find your mistakes

Reviewing your own games is the fastest way to improve. An engine shows in a minute where you went wrong and which move was best. All that is left is learning to read its hints.

How to load a game

  1. Open Game review.
  2. Load the game. Enter your Lichess or Chess.com username and pick a game — or paste a PGN (the game record).
  3. Start the analysis. The engine checks every move.
  4. Go through the mistakes. Start with the biggest ones — the blunders.

What the move labels mean

LabelWhat happened
BestYou played the engine's move.
GoodSlightly weaker than the best, but nothing spoiled.
InaccuracyYou lost a small part of your advantage.
MistakeYou made the position clearly worse.
BlunderYou gave away a piece, missed a mate or threw away a win.

Example: a trap the engine sees

The game starts 1. e4 e5 2. ♘f3 ♘c6 3. ♗c4 ♘d4. Black's knight seems to have left the e5-pawn hanging, and White wants to grab it.

After 4. ♘xe5? Black replies 4… ♕g5! — the queen attacks both the knight on e5 and the g2-pawn. If White stays greedy — 5. ♘xf7? ♕xg2 6. ♖f1 ♕xe4+ 7. ♗e2 — then 7… ♘f3# follows.

The evaluation graph

After the analysis you see a graph: the light area is White's advantage, the dark area Black's. Sharp drops are mistakes.

How to review a game usefully

  • Do it yourself first. Go through the game without the engine and mark the moments where you hesitated.
  • Look for the reason, not just the move. Ask yourself what you missed: a check, a capture or a threat?
  • Write down repeated mistakes. If you often hang pieces, solve hanging piece puzzles.
  • Check the opening. If the mistake came in the first moves, look it up in the openings guide.
