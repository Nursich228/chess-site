What the site can do

Explains moves in wordsThe coach tells you what the best move does — check, fork, sacrifice or threat — and what to expect from your opponent.
Stockfish 19 in your browserThe strongest chess engine runs on your device. No sign-up and no limits.
Rates your movesAfter every move you see whether it was best, an inaccuracy or a blunder, and what was better.
Game reviewLoad games from Lichess or Chess.com: accuracy, mistakes and a game graph.
Russian checkersOur own engine: compulsory captures, flying kings and the full capture path on the board.
Card counterMark which cards are out and always know which trumps are still in play.
Share a positionSend a friend a link and they open the same position.
